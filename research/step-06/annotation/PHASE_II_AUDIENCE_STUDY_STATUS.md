# Phase II audience study status

**Status: not executed.**

No participants have been recruited, no consent has been obtained and no institutional ethics determination has been recorded. Semantic-preservation annotators are not substitutes for end users. Synthetic or model-generated participants are prohibited.

Any later understanding or appropriate-reliance study requires, before recruitment:

- an applicable institutional ethics/consent determination;
- a defined participant population and recruitment process;
- preregistered comparison of proposed views with deterministic templates;
- evidence-based comprehension questions and appropriate-reliance tasks;
- separate measures for comprehension, decision behavior, satisfaction and time;
- disclosure that the tool is a research prototype, not clinical advice;
- a plan for correct and incorrect target suggestions without exposing participants to patient-care decisions.

Until actual data exist, no claim about improved understanding, usability, appropriate reliance, clinical utility or patient benefit is permitted.
